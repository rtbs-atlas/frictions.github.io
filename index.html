01 — 05 March 2027
Manchester


A Symposium on the Frictions of Science-Making across Ecology, Art, and Architecture.

This symposium puts a focal point on the frictions that arise when the ambitions of science-making (or ‘theory’, or ‘research’) come into contact with the disciplines of the arts and architecture . Centred around methodological questions, we also aim to extend our thought (and practice) into the philosophical and ethical domain.
